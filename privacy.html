Chrometry Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026.

Chrometry is a game and image colorimetry tool. Local palette extraction runs in your browser. Images are not uploaded for ordinary local palette analysis.

Pro AI

If you use Chrometry Pro AI, the selected image is transmitted to Chrometry's server-side AI endpoint so the requested analysis can be performed. The OpenAI API key is kept server-side.

Payments

Chrometry Pro payments and subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Chrometry does not receive or store your full card number through the application.

Advertising

The free website may display Google AdSense advertising. Google and other advertising technology providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising. Your choices may affect whether advertising is personalized.

Chrome extension

The extension does not contain Google AdSense. It uses the minimum browser permissions needed for its colorimetry functionality and may contact the Chrometry API when you use Pro features or subscription verification.

Information we may process

Depending on the feature you use, this can include an uploaded image, color-analysis results, a Pro activation token, and Stripe customer/subscription identifiers. We do not intentionally collect browsing history as a product feature.

Your choices

You can use local analysis without sending an image to the AI service. You can cancel a Pro subscription through the Stripe Customer Portal. For privacy questions, use the Contact page.

Chrometry Terms

Service

Chrometry provides browser-based image and game colorimetry tools. Local analysis is provided for general informational and creative-development use.

Pro subscription

Chrometry Pro is a recurring subscription processed through Stripe. Pro features include server-backed Scene Look AI and other premium functionality described in the product interface. Prices, billing interval, taxes, and applicable promotions are shown at checkout.

Cancellation

You may manage or cancel your subscription through the Stripe Customer Portal. Access to paid features follows the subscription status returned by Stripe.

AI limitations

AI output is an approximation based on the supplied image and available public information. It may be incorrect and should not be treated as a guarantee of proprietary game-engine settings, shaders, textures, LUTs, or other hidden technical details.

User content

You are responsible for having the rights and permissions needed to upload images and request analysis.
